IMDb import

Lets you paste an IMDb link in the entry form to auto-fill the title and cover. IMDb itself doesn't offer a public API, so this uses The Movie Database (TMDB), a free, community-run service — grab your own key at themoviedb.org/settings/api and paste it below. Nothing is sent anywhere except TMDB's servers, and only when you tap Fetch. This key also powers the Home page's search and recommendation rows for movies and series.

Book search

Powers book search and recommendations on the Home page. Works out of the box using Open Library (free, no key). Add a free Google Books key at console.cloud.google.com/apis/library/books.googleapis.com for richer results — ratings, descriptions, and better cover art.

Covers

New covers (camera, gallery, paste, TMDB import) are automatically cropped to a consistent poster shape and compressed before they're saved. Covers added before this existed are only cropped visually on screen — this reprocesses and re-saves them the same way, which also shrinks how much space they take up.
